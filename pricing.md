# Pricing — Mergiato

Mergiato is a desktop app for GitHub pull requests. Website: https://mergiato.com/pricing/

## Beta (current)
- Price: $0
- Limits: none; every feature is included
- Sign-up: none (no account, no card); it signs in through the user's own GitHub CLI (`gh`)
- Platforms: macOS today, Apple silicon and Intel (one universal download, under 10 MB)
- Updates: automatic and signed

## Later
- Paid plans may come after the beta, likely a one-time license rather than a subscription.
- Early users hear first, with fair notice. No price has been set.

Download: https://github.com/alex-parra/mergiato-pull-request-app/releases/latest
