Questions

Why is it free?

It's a beta. The most useful thing you can give back right now is feedback: what's missing, what breaks, what you'd pay for. Say it on GitHub issues.

Is there a catch with my data?

No. Mergiato has no servers and no account, and the app collects no analytics. Here's exactly what it can access.

Is there a team plan?

Not needed: each person installs it and signs in with their own GitHub CLI. Everything they do lands on GitHub as usual, so the rest of the team doesn't have to switch.
